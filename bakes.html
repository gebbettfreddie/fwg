A note on faithfulness. Every recipe is transcribed from the reel's own caption (or, for the cookies, the free PDF the baker links in her bio, and for the honey butter rolls, the baker's own recipe page), lightly tidied for reading. Nothing is invented. Every ingredient amount is in grams or millilitres. Where a baker used ounces, cups or spoons, those have been converted and rounded (for example, 1 tsp became 5 ml, and dry spoon measures became grams). All temperatures are in °C, converted and rounded where the baker used °F.

About the slider. It scales each ingredient by batch, from a half batch up to 4×, and shows the rough yield. Weights are rounded for the kitchen, so sanity-check at the extremes. A few amounts stay fixed on purpose, such as the salt-bread butter block, which is per piece. Scaling bread this far can shift timings and hydration feel, so treat large multiples as a starting point.
